## Setup

Complete graphs of N = 16 to 256 agents, constant source, tempered selection over 16 temperatures, κ ∈ {0, 0.3}, ε = 1,
1500 rounds, 2 repeats, GPU. Script: `python -m nichenet.experiments.e2`. Data: `data/site/e2.json`.

## Results

- **Exact agreement.** Final source attention equals x* = u*/(u* + N − 2), u* = ρ^{1/T_eff}, with a largest deviation of
  8·10⁻¹⁶ over 320 runs. The network settles into the symmetric state, where all relays are equivalent and the effective
  distance is the same for every relay at any δ (A1).
- **One curve.** With T_eff = (T − κ)/(1 − κ), attention crosses ½ at z = 1.00–1.02 for every N: the crossover sits at
  T_eff = ln ρ / ln(N − 2) ([Figure 3.5](/regimes/#boundaries)).
