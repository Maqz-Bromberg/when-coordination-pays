## Setup

The binary environment of Lee, Flack & Krakauer (h₀ = 0.2, τ_c = 1000, v = 0.1, |α| = 0.95) with a mean-field
population, for passive agents (control), stabilizers and destabilizers, at baseline τ_E = 100 and 1000.

1. **Residents:** for each of 12 memories τ_m from 0.3 to 60, a pure resident population gives the effective switching
   time τ_eff(τ_m), from the flip count.
2. **Best response:** a rare invader of the same type (1%) best-responds over the same grid, 2 repeats of 10⁷ steps.
   BR(τ_m) is the memory with the lowest invader-minus-resident divergence.
3. **Iteration:** τ_m → BR(τ_m) is iterated from both ends of the grid.

Script: `python -m nichenet.experiments.e4`. Data: `data/site/e4.json`.

## Results

| type, τ_E | τ_eff over residents | stable memory (BR slope) | τ_eff there | passive optimum at that τ_eff | best for the group |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| passive, 100 | 100 | 0.70 (0) | 100 | 0.61–0.93 | 0.79 |
| passive, 1000 | 1004 | 2.50 (0) | 1004 | 2.3–3.7 | 2.06 |
| stabilizer, 100 | 812–1631 | 3.12 (+0.12) | 1482 | 3.10 | 3.33 |
| stabilizer, 1000 | 8196–18831 | 10.73 (+0.05) | 18194 | 10.80 | 14.1 |
| destabilizer, 100 | 53–69 | 0.55 (0) | 53 | 0.65 | 0.79 |
| destabilizer, 1000 | 519–550 | 1.77 (−0.01) | 521 | 1.86 | 2.06 |

- **Stable in every case.** Iteration converges from both ends to the same memory, |BR slope| ≤ 0.12.
- **Self-consistent.** At the balance, the memory equals a passive agent's optimum for the environment the population
  creates: within 1% for stabilizers and 5–15% for destabilizers.
- **Stabilizers slow their world 15–18-fold** and settle at memories several times longer than passive agents;
  destabilizers halve τ_eff and settle shorter.
- **Free-riding.** The stable memory is shorter than the one that minimizes the residents' own divergence.
