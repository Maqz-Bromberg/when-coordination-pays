## Setup

Noisy news as in [E1](/appendix/e1/) (ν = 0.5, source moving with mean dwell τ_E), 30 agents, tempered selection
T = 0.3, κ = 0, ε = 1 so that memory is short and the window does the averaging. Windows of k = 1 to 64 signals,
3 repeats, scored by log-loss on the true source. Two pooling rules: sum the effective distances from the k seeded
agents, or average the k rewards. Script: `python -m nichenet.experiments.e7 [distances|rewards]`. Data:
`data/site/e7.json`, `data/site/e7_rewards.json`.

## Results

| τ_E | 10 | 30 | 100 | 300 | 1000 | slope |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| best k, summing distances | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | none |
| best k, averaging rewards | 1 | 1.6 | 2.7 | 4.1 | 7.1 | 0.42 (local 0.39–0.46) |

- **Summing distances never helps.** The true source stands out through the first-hand advantage ρ, which appears only
  in rounds where it is seeded. Summing distances over noisy rounds dilutes that contrast, and updating every k rounds
  also stretches the memory k-fold.
- **Averaging rewards is a sampling window,** with an optimum that grows as τ_E^{0.42}, close to the square-root law
  ([E1](/appendix/e1/)).
