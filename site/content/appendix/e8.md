result: A hub costs about twice as much as no structure at every group size (1.8–2.0× in broadcast dissipation), while its benefit grows like ln(N − 1). ## Setup

Complete graphs of N ∈ {10, 20, 40, 80}, tempered selection T ∈ {0.15, …, 0.8}, κ = 0.2, ε = 1; source moving with mean
dwell τ_E = 300, news landing on a random agent with probability ν ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75}. 3000 rounds, 10 snapshots,
2 repeats, GPU. Benefit is ln(N − 1) − log-loss; cost is broadcast dissipation relative to evenly spread attention
([chapter 2](/measures/)). Script: `python -m nichenet.experiments.e8`. Data: `data/site/e8.json`.

## Cost and benefit

| N | benefit / ln(N−1), ν = 0 / 0.5 / 0.75 | cost, ν = 0 / 0.75 | benefit per cost, ν = 0 / 0.75 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.99 / 0.94 / 0.67 | 1.79 / 1.47 | 1.21 / 1.00 |
| 20 | 0.98 / 0.95 / 0.69 | 1.88 / 1.69 | 1.54 / 1.20 |
| 40 | 0.99 / 0.98 / 0.74 | 1.92 / 1.42 | 1.90 / 1.91 |
| 80 | 0.98 / 0.96 / 0.75 | 1.91 / 1.42 | 2.26 / 2.30 |

- **A hub costs about 2× evenly spread attention, almost independent of N.** Benefit grows as ln(N − 1), so nats per unit
  of cost nearly double from 10 to 80 agents.
- **Noise lowers what is bought, not the cost.** Up to ν = 0.5 at most 6% of the benefit is lost; at ν = 0.75 a quarter
  to a third, and the cost drops with commitment.
- **Noise is paid in commitment.** The best T moves from 0.30 to 0.25, toward κ: the attention counterpart of the longer
  memory noise selects ([E1](/appendix/e1/)).
- **Below T = κ the full price buys nothing:** stale hubs cost 1.9–2.0 at every ν.

## The window under noise

| N | T½ (formula) | where tracking falls to ½ at ν = 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 | relay-aware walk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.568 | 0.494 / 0.389 / 0.274 | 0.477 / 0.367 / – |
| 20 | 0.464 | 0.409 / 0.337 / 0.251 | 0.403 / 0.325 / – |
| 40 | 0.410 | 0.374 / 0.313 / < 0.25 | 0.363 / 0.305 / 0.225 |
| 80 | 0.375 | 0.339 / 0.294 / < 0.25 | 0.338 / 0.291 / 0.234 |

- **The window splits.** Just above κ is a noise-robust band: at T = 0.25 tracking stays at 0.91–0.99 up to ν = 0.5 for
  every N ([chapter 5](/robustness/)). Toward T½ is a noise-sensitive band, where tracking follows the noisy mean field.
- **Noise removes a similar fraction of the window at every N:** about 20% at ν = 0.25, half at 0.5, 80% at 0.75.
- **Below κ the signal does not matter:** lock-in is set by the starting attention (A3).

## Shape along the way

Seed-averaged hierarchy coordinates along each variable (`python -m nichenet.experiments.paths`, `data/site/paths.json`;
[Figure 8.4](/structure/#paths)):

- **Commitment is a one-dimensional descent** from the hierarchy corner to about (0.25, 0.25, 0.25) past T½.
- **Shape cannot tell hierarchy from lock-in:** a stale hub is as tree-like as a hub on the source.
- **Noise erodes feedforwardness first:** the hub holds (treeness 0.88) while rivals rewire into loops (F 0.88 →
  0.57–0.78).
